## Defect and fix

Entering **Edit feature** on a phone started the shared edit session, opened Feature Details, and then a later visibility synchronization reopened the Draw bottom sheet. The two sheets occupied most of 390×844, clipped the attribute workflow, and presented unrelated geometry actions during attribute editing.

`setEditPanelVisibility` now treats an open mobile Feature Details drawer as an exclusive surface. Every attempted Draw-sheet reopen is rejected until the details drawer is actually closed; the existing close path then restores Draw tools when an editable selection remains. Focus still moves to the first writable attribute.

Failure evidence: `screenshots/stacked-mobile-edit-sheets-production-2026-08-11.png`.

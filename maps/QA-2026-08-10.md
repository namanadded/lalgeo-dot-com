# Help Center journey QA

## Human inspection

1. Open Maps at 390 × 844, 768 × 1024, and 1280 × 720.
2. Open the LalGeo menu, expand **Settings & Help** when shown, and choose **Help Center**.
3. Confirm the phone presentation is a bottom sheet and the tablet/desktop presentation stays fully in bounds.
4. Confirm focus starts in **What do you need help with?** and the close control is at least 44 × 44 px.
5. Choose **Import data**. Confirm the search is populated, the polite status reports the filtered guide count, and matching guides use collapsed disclosure rows.
6. Expand **Import layer** and confirm its guidance is readable without exposing keyword or implementation notes.
7. Search for `zzzz`. Confirm the plain **No guide found** recovery state appears.
8. Choose **Clear**. Confirm all guides return and focus remains in search.
9. Press Escape. Confirm Help Center closes and focus returns to the visible LalGeo menu button.
10. Reopen Help Center, Tab through every control, and confirm focus stays within the dialog until it is closed.

## Coverage gap and change

Production was healthy, but six existing core regression scripts were not required by `Production CI`: desktop project header, desktop drawing, mobile tool experience, mobile sheet refinement, toolbar interaction state, and large API point performance. A green local run could therefore hide a regression in those journeys.

All six are now explicit CI requirements. A new meta-regression compares every `test:*` package script with the workflow, fails on missing, duplicate, or unknown commands, and establishes the complete 74-test inventory as a durable contract. This prevents future journey coverage from silently becoming orphaned.

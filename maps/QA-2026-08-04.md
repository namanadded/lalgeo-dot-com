## Defect and fix

`Get Directions` previously left users without progress feedback, allowed repeated submissions, displayed a blocking browser alert on routing failure, and could render an older callback after a newer destination request.

The fixed journey now:

- keeps the selected place visible while adding a polite, atomic loading/error status;
- disables and marks the invoking button busy during the request;
- reuses the bounded location acquisition path;
- ignores superseded location and route completions;
- offers persistent Try again and Close controls;
- restores focus to the invoking directions button when the status closes;
- treats empty route geometry as a recoverable failure instead of a console-only warning.

## Human inspection

1. In the deploy preview, search for a place and open its result.
2. Choose **Get Directions** without granting location access. Confirm the place remains visible, the button becomes disabled/busy, and a persistent loading state appears.
3. Deny or time out location. Confirm **Directions unavailable**, **Try again**, and **Close** appear without a browser alert; Close should restore focus to **Get Directions**.
4. Grant location in a disposable browser profile and request directions. Confirm only the newest request can replace the route and route summary.
5. Repeat at 1440×900, 768×1024, and 390×844, checking clipping, overlap, focus rings, and the sidebar close path.
6. Confirm no project or Dropbox content is created or overwritten.

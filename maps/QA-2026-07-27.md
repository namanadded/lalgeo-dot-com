# Location journey QA — 2026-07-27

## Journey

Occasional users can now request their location without an indefinite browser watch or interrupting alerts. The same bounded request is reused when directions need an origin.

## Human inspection

1. Open Maps at 390 × 844, 768 × 1024, and 1280 × 720.
2. Close the welcome panel, then choose **Map → My Location** on phone/tablet or the locate control on desktop.
3. While the browser request is pending, confirm the card says **Finding your location…** and does not cover the toolbar.
4. Allow access and confirm the position is marked, the map centers once, and **Recenter** works.
5. Dismiss the card with its close button and repeat with `Escape`; focus should return to the locate control.
6. Block location access and retry. Confirm the card explains how to recover and **Try again** repeats the bounded request.
7. Search for a place, open it, and choose **Get Directions** with no cached location. Confirm it uses the same progress/error journey before routing.
